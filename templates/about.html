About SmartHire AI

SmartHire AI is an intelligent recruitment platform that automates resume screening using Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning. Recruiters post jobs, candidates upload resumes, and our AI engine extracts skills, calculates match scores, and ranks applicants — saving hours of manual review.

Our Mission

Make hiring faster and fairer through transparent, explainable AI scoring.

Tech Stack

Flask, MySQL, Scikit-learn, NLTK, PyPDF2, and Bootstrap 5.

Built For

Candidates, recruiters, and admins, with role-based dashboards for each.
