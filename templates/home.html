How the AI Scoring Works

Every uploaded resume is parsed, cleaned, and matched against job requirements using a transparent formula:

Resume Score = (Matching Skills ÷ Required Skills) × 100

This is blended with a TF-IDF cosine-similarity score for a more nuanced ranking signal, then candidates are automatically ordered on the recruiter's leaderboard.
